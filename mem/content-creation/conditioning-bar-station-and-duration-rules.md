---
name: Bar/station movements and duration honesty
description: Bar, dip-station, hanging and landing-dependent movements are illegal in conditioning and dynamic formats; advertised duration must match prescribed work within 10 minutes.
type: feature
---
Conditioning families (CARDIO, METABOLIC, CALORIE BURNING, CHALLENGE) and every dynamic format (CIRCUIT / EMOM / FOR TIME / AMRAP / TABATA) forbid movements that need a pull-up bar, dip station, rings/straps, inverted-row setup, hyperextension/roman chair, or a controlled landing: pull-ups, chin-ups (incl. gorilla chin, gironda sternum chin), dips, hanging leg/knee raises, muscle-ups, inverted/ring rows, box jump down with stabilization. Standing dynamic work (swings, snatches, cleans, thrusters, standing presses, burpees, jump squats, skaters) stays legal.

Duration honesty: prescribed work (Main + Finisher) must be within 10 minutes of the advertised session in BOTH directions. Over 10 min over or under = hard error, not a warning. Section headers that declare their own clock ("Main Workout (EMOM 25')", "Finisher (10 minutes)", "15-minute AMRAP") fix the cost of that block in the estimator.

Training programs are audited day by day too: every Main Workout day needs 3+ linked library exercises and no Finisher may be vague prose without linked exercises.
