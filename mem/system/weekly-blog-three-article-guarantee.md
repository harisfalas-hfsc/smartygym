---
name: Weekly blog three-article guarantee
description: How the weekly Fitness/Nutrition/Wellness blog articles are scheduled and the backfill safety net that guarantees all three.
type: feature
---
Three articles per week, one each for Fitness, Nutrition, Wellness, generated by `generate-weekly-blog-articles`.

Staggered primary cron (UTC, Monday): Fitness 04:00, Nutrition 04:10, Wellness 04:20 — one category per invocation so the 115s deadline guard can never drop the last category. Wellness simply appears ~20 minutes after Fitness; that is not a failure.

Safety net (added Sept 2026): `generate-weekly-blog-articles-backfill-mon` (Mon 07,11,15,19,23 UTC) and `generate-weekly-blog-articles-backfill-tue` (Tue 03,07,11 UTC) call the function with all three categories and `skipExistingThisWeek: true`. Existing categories are skipped with zero AI spend; only a genuinely missing category is regenerated. This covers weeks where the function itself failed silently (e.g. 2026-08-24 and 2026-08-31 produced nothing despite cron succeeding).

Never remove the stagger or the backfill jobs.
